Kebijakan Privasi Finora

Berlaku sejak 26 Oktober 2026 (versi 2026-10-26). Dokumen ini berisi draf yang belum ditinjau ahli hukum.

1. Siapa kami

Finora adalah aplikasi pencatat keuangan pribadi untuk Android. Pengelola dan Pengendali Data Pribadi adalah pengembang Finora (Yehuda Hero Setiawan, perorangan). Kontak: yahudahero168@gmail.com.

2. Data apa yang kami proses

Kami tidak mengumpulkan lokasi, kontak, foto, atau isi Drive-mu selain spreadsheet yang dibuat Finora sendiri.

3. Izin Google yang diminta

Finora memakai masuk dengan Google (email dan profil dasar) dan satu izin Drive: drive.file. Izin ini hanya membuat Finora dapat membuat dan membuka file yang dibuat oleh Finora sendiri (spreadsheet “Finora - nama buku”). Finora tidak dapat melihat file Drive-mu yang lain.

4. Pernyataan Limited Use Google

Penggunaan dan pemindahan informasi yang diterima Finora dari Google API akan mematuhi Kebijakan Data Pengguna Layanan Google API, termasuk persyaratan Limited Use. Data dari Google hanya dipakai untuk menyediakan fitur yang terlihat di aplikasi, tidak dijual, tidak dipakai untuk iklan, dan tidak dibaca manusia kecuali atas izinmu, untuk keamanan, atau bila diwajibkan hukum.

5. Dasar pemrosesan

Persetujuanmu saat masuk (dicatat tanggal dan versinya), serta pelaksanaan layanan yang kamu minta (mencatat keuanganmu). Kamu dapat menarik persetujuan kapan saja dengan menghapus akun (bagian 8).

6. Pihak ketiga dan transfer ke luar negeri

Kami memakai Google (masuk dengan Google, Sheets dan Drive API, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Analytics). Server Google dapat berada di luar Indonesia. Kami memilih lokasi database Firestore di Jakarta (asia-southeast2), tetapi layanan Google lain dapat memproses data di negara lain dengan perlindungan sebagaimana kebijakan Google. Kami tidak menjual data kepada siapa pun.

7. Berapa lama disimpan

Profil Firebase disimpan selama akunmu aktif dan dihapus saat kamu meminta penghapusan. Data Analytics mengikuti pengaturan masa simpan Firebase. Catatan keuangan ada di Drive-mu dan tetap ada sampai kamu menghapusnya sendiri.

8. Hak kamu dan cara memakainya

  • Akses dan salinan: catatan uangmu sudah ada di spreadsheet Drive-mu. Untuk salinan profil, kirim email.
  • Perbaikan: ubah catatanmu langsung di aplikasi; untuk profil, kirim email.
  • Mematikan analitik: Pengaturan > Privasi.
  • Penghapusan data: kirim email ke yahudahero168@gmail.com dari alamat akun Google yang kamu pakai dengan subjek “Hapus akun”. Kami menghapus profil Firebase dalam 30 hari. Spreadsheet di Drive-mu tidak ikut terhapus; hapus sendiri dari Google Drive bila perlu. Kamu juga bisa mencabut akses Finora di myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Keamanan dan insiden

Aturan Firestore membatasi setiap pengguna hanya pada profilnya sendiri dan hanya pada kolom hitungan yang disebut di atas. Bila terjadi kebocoran data pribadi, kami memberi tahu pengguna dan pihak berwenang dalam 3 x 24 jam sesuai UU PDP.

10. Anak-anak

Finora ditujukan untuk pengguna berusia 17 tahun ke atas.

11. Perubahan kebijakan

Bila isi berubah, kami menaikkan versi dan meminta persetujuan baru di aplikasi. Tanggal berlaku ada di atas halaman ini.

Finora Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 (version 2026-10-26). This is a draft that has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Who we are

Finora is a personal finance tracking app for Android. The controller of personal data is the Finora developer (Yehuda Hero Setiawan, an individual). Contact: yahudahero168@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

DataWhere it is storedWhy
Financial entries: amounts, notes, categories, budgetsYour own Google Sheets/Drive, and a cache on your phoneTo run the tracking features. Never sent to Finora servers.
Google account email and nameFirebase Firestore (Google Cloud, Jakarta location), one profile document per userTo recognise your account, support, and count users
Usage counts: number of entries, books, budgets, language, app version, last active timeThe same Firebase profileTo understand usage and improve the app. Contains no amounts or notes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or Drive contents other than the spreadsheets Finora itself creates.

3. Google permissions requested

Finora uses Google Sign-In (email and basic profile) and one Drive permission: drive.file. It lets Finora create and open only the files Finora itself created (the “Finora - book name” spreadsheets). Finora cannot see your other Drive files.

4. Google Limited Use statement

Finora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features visible in the app, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your consent, for security, or where required by law.

5. Legal basis

Your consent when signing in (recorded with date and version), and performing the service you asked for (tracking your finances). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (section 8).

6. Third parties and international transfer

We use Google (Sign-In, Sheets and Drive APIs, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Analytics). Google servers may be outside Indonesia. We chose the Firestore database location in Jakarta (asia-southeast2), but other Google services may process data in other countries under Google's own safeguards. We do not sell data to anyone.

7. How long we keep it

The Firebase profile is kept while your account is active and deleted when you request deletion. Analytics data follows the Firebase retention setting. Your financial entries live in your Drive until you delete them yourself.

8. Your rights and how to use them

  • Access and copy: your money entries are already in your Drive spreadsheet. For a copy of your profile, email us.
  • Correction: edit your entries in the app; for the profile, email us.
  • Turn off analytics: Settings > Privacy.
  • Deletion: email yahudahero168@gmail.com from the Google account address you use, subject “Delete account”. We delete the Firebase profile within 30 days. The spreadsheets in your Drive are not deleted; delete them yourself in Google Drive if you wish. You can also revoke Finora's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Security and incidents

Firestore rules restrict each user to their own profile and only the count fields listed above. If a personal data breach happens, we notify users and the authorities within 3 x 24 hours as required by Indonesia's PDP Law.

10. Children

Finora is intended for users aged 17 and over.

11. Changes

If the content changes we raise the version and ask for new consent in the app. The effective date is at the top of this page.
